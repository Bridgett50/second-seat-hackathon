## What the unit tests prove (in-memory scaffold only)

`services/bot/test/meeting-connector.test.ts` (run with `SECOND_SEAT_TEST_MODE=1`) proves, against the in-memory connector in `services/bot/src/meeting/connector.ts`:

- Work is honored only through a server-issued job (`issueJob` binds owner, meeting, session, source revision, authority generation under a server-generated jobId). Caller-built callbacks without an issued jobId are rejected by `resolveCallback` and `replay`.
- Source edits, revocation, deletion, expiry, and session suspension invalidate dependent pending jobs immediately (no debounce).
- Duplicate resolution of one job yields one inbox artifact; replaying one dispatch intent invokes the transport at most once. Distinct issued jobs each work.
- The in-process fake adapter invokes the injected transport exactly once and propagates its errors; `http://127.0.0.1`, `http://localhost`, and bracketed `http://[::1]` each invoke the injected stub once, while non-loopback hosts, malformed URLs, unsupported schemes, and malformed fake targets invoke it zero times and throw `MeetingDenied`.
- `apps/web/src/server.ts` does not import the meeting connector (asserted by the `T1 regression` test, which reads that file).

## What is missing for integration

These unit tests prove nothing about authenticated routes, saved workspaces, full network blocking, live models, or any real provider. No real HTTP is performed and no redirect is followed by the stub gate.
