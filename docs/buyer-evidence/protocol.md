# Buyer Evidence Protocol

## Purpose

Learn whether Second Seat helps the intended buyer review vendor calls faster and with fewer errors, and what confuses them. This is early usability evidence, not proof of demand.

## Intended buyer

Small-business owner or operations lead who negotiates with vendors and reviews vendor call notes before replying.

## Task

Review a made-up vendor meeting transcript, identify offers, asks, and terms needing a decision, and write a reply they would send.

## Method

Each participant completes the task twice, once per condition:

- A: their existing process (their usual notes, email, or manual review, without Second Seat).
- B: Second Seat (offline demo at /session).

The two runs use equivalent but different scenarios, matched in difficulty. Task order and scenario assignment are rotated across participants (counterbalanced), so some do A then B and others do B then A, with scenarios assigned evenly across orders.

## Measures

Per condition, record: time on task, factual mistakes in the reply, missed terms from the answer key, confusion points raised or observed, and whether the participant would use it again.

## Roles

Bridgett handles recruitment, observation, and recording. Second Seat and its agents do not contact participants automatically (no automatic contact); all outreach is done by Bridgett directly.

## Consent and data

Participation is voluntary with informed consent. Sessions may be observed and recorded by Bridgett for note-taking. Data is synthetic only: scenarios are fictional, and participants must not paste real meetings or real vendor details.

## Honesty rules

Do not invent observations, quotes, savings, or willingness to pay. Record only what happened. Failures and negative observations are recorded and reported alongside positive ones. Verbatim quotes only; leave blank when there is none.

## Sample size

Results are a small usability sample (for example, 3 to 6 people), not market validation.

## Reporting rules

- Report per-session results using the blank evidence template, one form per session.
- Keep observed facts, participant quotes, and founder interpretation in separate sections.
- Report time, factual mistakes, missed terms, confusion points, and would-use-again answers for both conditions.
- Report negative observations and failures; do not drop sessions with poor outcomes.
- Summaries state the sample size and that this is not market validation.
