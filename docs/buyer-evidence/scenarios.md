# Practice Scenarios

All scenarios below are made-up and fictional, written for this protocol. They are not drawn from sealed or held-out data. Company names and people are fictional; any resemblance is coincidental. No real people, no PII.

The three scenarios are matched in difficulty: each has one vendor offer, one vendor ask, one deadline, and one price change, plus one distractor detail that needs no decision.

## Scenario 1 — Office supply restock call

Transcript (fictional vendor call, Paperline Supply Co.):

- Dana (Paperline): Thanks for the call. We can offer next-day delivery on copy paper if you order by Thursday noon each week.
- Owner: We go through a lot on Mondays. What do you need from us?
- Dana: Just one ask: confirm your monthly box count by the first of each month so we reserve stock.
- Dana: Heads-up on terms: the per-box price rises from 40 to 44 starting next month.
- Dana: Also, we repainted our delivery vans blue this spring.
- Owner: I will check our usage and reply by Friday.

Answer key (4 terms):

1. Offer: next-day delivery on copy paper for orders placed by Thursday noon.
2. Ask: confirm monthly box count by the first of each month.
3. Deadline: reply on usage by Friday.
4. Price change: per-box price rises from 40 to 44 starting next month.

## Scenario 2 — Cleaning service renewal call

Transcript (fictional vendor call, Brightstep Cleaning):

- Lee (Brightstep): Good morning. We can offer a Saturday deep-clean slot once a month at no extra visit fee.
- Owner: Saturdays could work. What do you need from me?
- Lee: One ask: send us your alarm code update before the first Saturday visit.
- Lee: On terms: the monthly rate rises from 300 to 330 starting in two months.
- Lee: Also, our team just got new uniforms.
- Owner: I will confirm the alarm code and the renewal by Wednesday.

Answer key (4 terms):

1. Offer: monthly Saturday deep-clean slot with no extra visit fee.
2. Ask: send the updated alarm code before the first Saturday visit.
3. Deadline: confirm renewal by Wednesday.
4. Price change: monthly rate rises from 300 to 330 starting in two months.

## Scenario 3 — Produce delivery terms call

Transcript (fictional vendor call, Greenharbor Produce):

- Sam (Greenharbor): Hello. We can offer twice-weekly delivery, Tuesday and Friday mornings.
- Owner: Friday mornings are tight. What do you need from us?
- Sam: One ask: place Friday orders by Wednesday evening so we can pack the truck.
- Sam: On terms: the per-crate price rises from 25 to 28 starting next quarter.
- Sam: Also, we sponsored a neighborhood fun run last month.
- Owner: I will review the schedule and answer by Monday.

Answer key (4 terms):

1. Offer: twice-weekly delivery on Tuesday and Friday mornings.
2. Ask: place Friday orders by Wednesday evening.
3. Deadline: answer on the schedule by Monday.
4. Price change: per-crate price rises from 25 to 28 starting next quarter.
