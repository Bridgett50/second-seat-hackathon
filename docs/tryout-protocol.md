# Buyer tryout protocol — Friday, October 30, 2026

Method for the Friday buyer tryouts. This file does not record results. Founder story, user observations, and video URLs stay pending until a session is written up from what actually happened.

No mentor is named in this protocol.

## Who runs it

Bridgett recruits, hosts, and writes the notes. Second Seat does not contact anyone.

## Data

Synthetic meeting data only. Use the fictional scenarios in [buyer-evidence/scenarios.md](buyer-evidence/scenarios.md). Participants do not paste a real meeting, a real vendor, or any real personal details.

## Consent

Participation is voluntary. Before the session, say what will happen, that they can stop at any time, and that notes may include one short quote only if they agree. Do not record a session, or keep a quote, without that permission.

## What is measured

Each person does two passes on equivalent fictional meetings, in rotated order, following [buyer-evidence/protocol.md](buyer-evidence/protocol.md):

- Their usual review, without Second Seat.
- The offline demo at `/session` (demo mode; no live model call).

Record, per pass: time on task, factual mistakes in the reply, missed terms against the answer key, and confusion points. This is a small usability sample. It is not market validation.

## One quote

Ask once, after the session, whether they will allow one short quote. If they agree, write that quote verbatim and mark it as permitted. If they do not agree, leave the quote blank. Do not invent a quote, a name, or a result.

## Afterward

Fill one [buyer-evidence/evidence-template.md](buyer-evidence/evidence-template.md) per session. Leave empty fields empty.
